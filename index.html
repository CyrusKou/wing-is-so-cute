Exercise 9 – Editing Paragraph Instructions

Original Passage: Autonomous Vehicles
1Autonomous vehicles are cars and trucks equipped with sensors and software to drive themselves. 2John Smith wrote in Wired magazine on May 10, 2022. 3John Smith describes a massive new effort among tech giants, traditional automakers, and city planners to create vehicles that can navigate traffic, avoid pedestrians, or reduce emissions. 4Self-driving cars could offer significant benefits in many cities. 5For instance, delivery vans that can be customized could detect if a route is congested and then find a faster path. 6Commuter shuttles could help to revitalize the struggling public transportation sector. 7It has suffered budget cuts as a result of the way cities are expanding outward. 8Autonomous technology could help to revitalize the struggling public transportation sector. 9Smith writes that self-driving buses could "propel urban transit into the modern era." 10They could reduce traffic fatalities in the process. 11The author warns that several questions remain regarding the safety, regulation, maintenance, and use of autonomous vehicles. 12Skeptics worry about cybersecurity and it is possible that the cars could be hacked, like any computer system. 13However, I believe that self-driving cars have a big potential to transform our daily commutes and to modernize the transportation industry.
1. Change sentence 2 to a participial phrase beginning with Writing. Delete John Smith. Then combine sentences 2 and 3.
2. Summaries should include attribution to the author of the original piece. Insert According to Smith at the beginning of sentence 4.
3. In sentence 5, replace that can be customized with the adjective customizable. Place the adjective before delivery vans.
4. Transitions make writing more coherent. In sentence 8, add In addition or Also.
5. Combine sentences 8 and 9. Change sentence 9 into an adjective clause beginning with a comma and which.
6. In sentence 9, reduce wordiness by replacing the way cities are expanding outward with the noun suburbanization.
7. Combine sentences 10 and 11 by reducing sentence 11 to a verb phrase.
8. In sentence 12, replace use with usability.
9. In sentence 13, to improve parallel structure, change the phrase it is possible to to a noun form starting with the.
10. In sentence 14, replace big with a more powerful word like tremendous or enormous.
